## Art Style

**Chosen Style: Korean Manhwa (Dark Fantasy variant)**

### Why this style?

| Criteria | Justification |
|----------|---------------|
| **Market fit** | Korean Manhwa is the dominant vertical-scroll webtoon format. Dark fantasy variant (as seen in *Solo Leveling*, *Magic Emperor*, *The Beginning After the End*) is the highest-performing genre on major platforms. |
| **Tonal match** | The cultivation + shadow magic story demands dramatic lighting, sharp contrast, and moody atmospheres. Manhwa's signature soft shading with sharp rim lights perfectly captures this. |
| **Readability** | Manhwa's clean linework and balanced compositions ensure mobile-first readability even in dark scenes — critical for a shadow-based story. |
| **Production** | Soft-shaded manhwa renders faster than full painterly styles while maintaining a premium look. It scales well across 20+ panel episodes. |

### Style Signature

| Element | Choice |
|---------|--------|
| **Base** | Korean Manhwa soft-shaded |
| **Rendering** | Soft shading with hard rim light accents |
| **Linework** | Clean, medium-weight lines (1.5–3 px) |
| **Eyes** | Detailed, glossy — the primary emotional anchor |
| **Hair** | Defined strands with soft gradient, rim-lit |
| **Backgrounds** | Painted with atmospheric depth, not flat colors |

## Visual Theme

### Mood & Atmosphere
- **Mood:** Melancholic, mysterious, powerful but burdened
- **Atmosphere:** Heavy with destiny — shadows feel alive, light is earned
- **Tone:** Dark epic with moments of vibrant magic

### Rendering & Detail
| Element | Specification |
|---------|--------------|
| **Rendering Quality** | Premium — 85% clean finish, 15% painterly texture |
| **Line Style** | Clean manhwa lines — slightly thinner on faces (1 px), heavier on environments (2–3 px) |
| **Brush Style** | Solid base brush + soft airbrush for blending + hard round for rim lights |
| **Texture Amount** | Moderate — subtle paper grain on backgrounds, clean on characters |
| **Detail Level** | High on faces/eyes/magic effects, medium on environments, low on distant elements |
| **Edge Sharpness** | Soft interior edges, sharp silhouette edges (rim light creates the cutout) |

### Shadow & Light
| Element | Specification |
|---------|--------------|
| **Shadow Style** | Cool-toned shadows (shift toward deep teal/indigo) |
| **Depth Style** | Multi-plane depth — foreground silhouette → midground detailed → background atmospheric |
| **Perspective Style** | Dynamic — low angles for power shots, high angles for vulnerability, Dutch angles for tension |
| **Color Temperature** | Cool-tinted overall (5000–5500K) with warm accent lighting (magic, fire) |
| **Lighting Philosophy** | "The darker the shadow, the brighter the light" — high contrast with intentional highlights |

### Effects & Weather
| Element | Specification |
|---------|--------------|
| **FX Style** | Particle-based magic with drifting embers, shadow wisps, spirit energy trails |
| **Screen Tone Usage** | Minimal — used only for flashback sequences and dream sequences (grainy tone) |
| **Fog Usage** | Atmospheric fog in outdoor establishing shots; ground mist in spirit realms |
| **Bloom Usage** | Moderate — only on magic effects, energy sources, and eye highlights |
| **Motion Blur Usage** | Subtle directional blur on fast action panels (sword swings, dodges) |
| **Particle Effects** | Shadow dust (dark particles rising), spirit flames (jade-green wisps), falling petals (dramatic moments) |
| **Magic Effects** | Shadow tendrils (dark violet → black gradient), jade energy (teal-green glow), soul absorption (golden-violet swirl) |
| **Weather Effects** | Rain in emotional flashbacks, mist in forest scenes, clear but hazy in throne room scenes |

## Strict Color Rule: 60/30/10

### Application Breakdown

| Proportion | Role | Used For |
|------------|------|----------|
| **60% — Dominant** | Deep Midnight Blue / Shadow Black | Backgrounds, skies, architecture, ground, environment, large objects, walls, floors, large clothing areas, darkness |
| **30% — Secondary** | Weathered Silver / Pale Jade Gray | Characters, foreground, important props, hair (white/black), secondary clothing, trees, lighting support |
| **10% — Accent** | Jade Green / Spirit Gold | Eyes, magic, weapons, UI, energy, blood highlights, jewelry, important story objects, glow, fire, important effects |

## Color Continuity

### Immutable Rules

| Element | Fixed Color | Cannot Be |
|---------|-------------|-----------|
| **Shen Wei's eyes** | Jade Soul `#00D49E` | Never blue, red, or brown |
| **Shen Wei's robes** | Shadow Black `#0A0D14` with Jade clasps | Never white, red, or purple |
| **Shen Wei's hair** | Jet Black with Mist White `#D8DEE4` highlights | Never brown or blue |
| **Shadow cultivation** | Violet Arc `#8B5CF6` fading to black | Never golden or red |
| **Sacred cultivation** | Azure Core `#3B82F6` | Never violet or green |
| **Blood** | Crimson Wound `#E83030` | Never black, never pink |
| **Jade artifacts** | Jade Soul `#00D49E` | Never emerald green or teal |
| **Sunset** | Deep violet `#2D1B2E` → Warm orange `#F97316` | Never purple → pink |
| **Spirit energy** | Pale jade `#A7F3D0` | Never white or yellow |
| **Danger signals** | Crimson `#DC2626` | Never blue or green |

### Scene Transition Rules

- **Day → Night:** Shift overall palette by -40% brightness, +30% blue saturation. Preserve accent colors at full saturation.
- **Reality → Flashback:** Apply sepia-violet overlay, add 25% screen tone, reduce saturation by 40%.
- **Reality → Spirit Realm:** Apply violet push (+20% purple), add floating particle overlay, soften edges by 60%.
- **Neutral → Combat:** Increase contrast by 30%, deepen shadows to `#020408`, add motion lines.
- **Neutral → Victory:** Add warm golden light source, raise brightness by 20%, add ascending golden particles.
- **Cut to Villain:** Shift shadows toward crimson, add red rim light, increase contrast by 40%.

## Cinematic Color Grading

### Chosen Grade: Arcane (Riot Games) × Solo Leveling

**Why this blend?**

| Reason | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Dark fantasy fit** | Arcane's blue-shadow-dominant palette with isolated warm accents perfectly serves a shadow cultivation story |
| **Depth through color** | Arcane's technique of using complementary color pairs (blue/orange, violet/gold) creates visual hierarchy without relying on brightness alone |
| **Premium manhwa integration** | Solo Leveling's clean, high-contrast manhwa rendering combined with Arcane's painterly atmosphere produces a unique "premium indie" look |
| **Mobile readability** | Unlike pure Arcane (which relies on fine texture), the Solo Leveling influence ensures the grade reads clearly on small screens |
| **Distinct identity** | This hybrid grade differentiates the webtoon from the standard "purple-blue" dark fantasy look that dominates the market |

### Grade Signature

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Shadow Tone** | Cool blue-black (`#080D1A`) |
| **Midtone Balance** | Slightly cool with preserved skin warmth |
| **Highlight Tone** | Jade-tinted white (`#D8F0E8`) |
| **Overall Saturation** | 75% (slightly desaturated for mood) |
| **Black Point** | Raised slightly for manhwa readability (true black only in void scenes) |
| **White Point** | Never pure white (always tinted) |
| **Color Contrast** | Complementary pairs (blue-gold, teal-crimson, violet-amber) |

### Lut Lookup
```
Shadows:    rgb(10, 13, 20)  → rgb(8, 12, 26)    ← deeper blue
Midtones:   rgb(128, 128, 128) → rgb(120, 130, 140) ← cool shift
Highlights: rgb(230, 235, 240) → rgb(216, 240, 232) ← jade tint
```

## Quality Rules Checklist

### ✅ Every panel must satisfy:

| Rule | Verification Method |
|------|--------------------|
| **60/30/10 color balance** | Measure pixel proportions — accent MUST NOT exceed 10% |
| **Strong visual hierarchy** | Reader's eye is drawn to accent first, then secondary, then dominant |
| **Excellent readability** | All text has minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio against background |
| **Modern premium Webtoon look** | Clean linework, atmospheric depth, intentional color choices |
| **Mobile-friendly contrast** | Test at 360px width at 50% brightness — all elements still distinct |
| **Consistent color grading** | Shadow/midtone/highlight shift follows the Arcane × Solo Leveling grade |
| **Consistent lighting** | Light direction remains fixed within each scene; transitions are motivated |
| **Cinematic atmosphere** | Each panel could be a frame in an animated sequence |
| **No oversaturated colors** | Max saturation capped at 90% — only magic/blood at 100% |
| **No muddy shadows** | Shadows always have a clear color cast (blue, violet, or warm), never just black |
| **Accent colors used only for emphasis** | Accent NEVER used for large areas, background, or secondary surfaces |
| **High-end Webtoon Originals aesthetic** | Matches quality bar of top-tier platform Originals |

### ❌ Never Do:

| Violation | Why |
|-----------|-----|
| Randomly change character colors | Destroys character recognition and emotional continuity |
| Use accent colors for shading/shadows | Accent is for emphasis, not rendering |
| Let fog obscure readership | Text must always be legible through atmospheric effects |
| Use pure white for backgrounds | Always tint white toward the scene's dominant color temperature |
| Let magic effects overwhelm the composition | FX must enhance, not dominate — accent rule applies to effects too |
| Break the 60/30/10 rule for "cinematic" effect | Rules exist for consistency; breaks must have story-motivated justification |
